Introduction

Europe's best-kept secret
is finally getting noticed.

Slovenia sits tucked between Italy, Austria, Croatia, and Hungary — a country the size of New Jersey that somehow packs in alpine mountains, emerald lakes, medieval castles, Roman ruins, and a walkable capital city. Tourists who discover it call it the most beautiful country they've ever visited. The best part? It's still affordable, still uncrowded, and still genuinely welcoming. This 5-day guide gives you a complete, realistic plan for exploring Ljubljana and Lake Bled on a €500 total budget — including where to sleep, eat, and spend your time, day by day.

Where to Sleep

The best budget stays
in Ljubljana

All options are in or near the old town — walking distance to everything. Book in advance especially May–September.

Hostel Celica
Design Hostel — Best Pick
€22–28 / night dorm
📍 Metelkova, 10 min walk from centre
A converted 1882 military prison — cells turned into individually designed rooms by 80 Slovenian artists. Possibly the most unique hostel in Europe. Book early, it fills up fast.
Hostel Tresor
Central Hostel
€18–24 / night dorm
📍 Centre — right next to the market
Inside a converted old bank building. Social vibe, good bar downstairs, extremely central location — you can walk everywhere from here.

Budget Hotel — Private Room

If you're traveling as a couple and splitting the cost, this gets you a clean private room right in the old town for around €28–35 per person — worth it.
Day by Day

5 days, fully planned

Day 01
Arrival & The Old Town
~€42 Today's total
Morning
Arrive & Check In
Ljubljana Airport (LJU) → City Centre
Take the Arriva airport bus (line 28G) directly to the city — runs every hour, takes 45 min. Much cheaper than a taxi. Drop your bags and take a slow walk to the Ljubljanica River to orient yourself.
Bus: €4.10
💡 Buy a LPP city bus card at the airport or any newsstand. You'll use it all week.
Lunch
Odprta Kuhna (Open Kitchen)
Pogačarjev trg, Old Town (Fridays only — if not Friday: Pri Škofju)
Friday's Open Kitchen market is where Ljubljana chefs compete for your attention. Best street food in the city. Any other day, head to Pri Škofju for traditional Slovenian lunch — try the goveja juha (beef soup) and strukljii.
€8–12
Afternoon
Ljubljana Old Town Walk + Triple Bridge
Stari Trg → Prešeren Square → Tromostovje
Walk the entire old town — it's small enough to do in 2 hours. Cross the famous Triple Bridge, walk along the Ljubljanica riverfront, browse the Central Market. Free and genuinely beautiful.
Free
Dinner
Restavracija AS / Gostilna na Gradu
Old Town / Ljubljana Castle
For a budget-friendly first dinner, Zvezda Café does excellent local pastries and light meals. For something more substantial, Gostilna Dela near the market offers Slovenian mains for €10–14.
€12–16
Evening
Riverfront Drinks — Filozofska Kavarna or Minimal Bar
Breg (south bank of Ljubljanica)
The south bank of the Ljubljanica is lined with bars. Grab a local beer (Union or Laško) and watch the city settle into night.
€5–8
🚌 Transport tip: Everything in Ljubljana's old town is walkable. Save your bus card for trips to the outskirts. Taxis via Bolt app are cheap — €3–6 for most city rides.
Day 02
Ljubljana Deep Dive
~€38 Today's total
Morning
Ljubljana Castle
Grajska planota 1 — funicular or 20-min hike up
Take the funicular (€4 return, included with castle ticket) or hike up for free. The castle is worth it for the views alone — on clear days you see the Alps. Go early to beat crowds. The virtual museum inside is actually great.
€13 (castle + funicular)
💡 The hike up through the park is free and takes about 20 min. Skip the funicular and save €4 if you're fit.
Lunch
Covered Market — Central Market Hall
Vodnikov trg, Old Town
The covered market building along the river has cheap, excellent local food stalls. Grab a burek (€2.50), fresh fruit from vendors, or a proper Slovenian plate for under €7. Real local food, zero tourist markup.
€5–8
Afternoon
Metelkova Mesto + National Museum
Metelkova ulica + Trg Republike
Metelkova is Ljubljana's alternative arts quarter — a former Yugoslav army barracks turned into an open-air gallery with street art, sculptures, and small galleries. Then walk to the National Museum of Slovenia (free on first Sunday, otherwise €6) for Slovenian history and the famous Celtic situla.
€0–6
Dinner
Hot Horse — Local Institution
Tivoli Park area
Hot Horse has been serving Slovenian-style horse meat burgers since 1992. It sounds unusual but it's genuinely delicious, very cheap, and totally authentic. A Ljubljana rite of passage. If not your thing — Pizzeria Romeo nearby does proper wood-fired pizza for €9–12.
€5–10
Evening
Tivoli Park Evening Walk
Tivoli City Park — largest park in Ljubljana
A massive city park five minutes from the centre. Locals jog, walk dogs, and sit on benches here every evening. Completely free and genuinely lovely.
Free
🚶 Transport tip: Today is almost entirely walkable. Metelkova is 15 min walk from the old town. Tivoli is 10 min west of Prešeren Square.
Day 03
Lake Bled Day Trip
~€58 Today's total
Morning
Bus to Lake Bled + Bled Castle
Ljubljana Bus Station → Bled (55 min)
Arriva buses run regularly from Ljubljana's main bus station to Bled — buy tickets at the counter or onboard. Arrive early (before 10am) to beat the tour groups. Walk up to Bled Castle immediately for the iconic view before crowds arrive.
Bus: €7 return · Castle: €15
💡 Leave Ljubljana by 8am. The lake gets extremely crowded by midday in summer — early birds get magic hour light and empty paths.
Lunch
Penzion Mlino or Picerija Rustika
Bled lakeside
Avoid the overpriced restaurant terraces on the main strip. Penzion Mlino just south of the lake does good Slovenian lunch for €10–14. Even better — grab a kremšnita (Bled cream cake) from Slaščičarna Zima, the original recipe. You MUST try this. €3.50 for a slice of legend.
€10–14
Afternoon
Bled Island Pletna Boat + Walk Around the Lake
Bled Lake
The traditional wooden pletna boats row you to the island church — it's touristy but iconic. Budget tip: the walk around the entire lake takes 1h 30min and is completely free. Do the walk, swim if it's warm (the water is clean and free), then take one pletna at the end. Ring the church bell 3 times for a wish.
Pletna: €18 · Lake walk: Free
Dinner
Back to Ljubljana — Gostilna Sokol
Ciril-Metodov trg 18, Ljubljana
Take the 5–6pm bus back. Gostilna Sokol is an old-school Slovenian inn doing hearty traditional dishes — žganci (buckwheat porridge), štruklji (rolled dumplings), roast meats. Classic, cheap, real.
Bus: included · Dinner: €11–15
🚌 Transport tip: Arriva bus Ljubljana–Bled departs from the main bus station (Trg Osvobodilne fronte). Journey ~55 min. First bus ~6:30am, last return ~9pm. No booking needed, just show up.
Day 04
Postojna Cave & Predjama Castle
~€65 Today's total
Morning
Bus to Postojna + Cave Tour
Ljubljana → Postojna (1hr by bus)
Postojna Cave is one of the largest cave systems in the world — 24km of passages, an underground train, and the only place you'll see living olm (human fish). The 90-min guided tour takes you through extraordinary formations. Book the cave ticket online in advance to save time — prices are the same but you skip the queue.
Bus: €6.80 · Cave: €29.90
💡 Inside the cave it's 8°C year-round. Bring a light jacket regardless of outside temperature.
Lunch
Gostilna Požar (Postojna town)
Postojna town centre, 10 min walk from cave
Skip the overpriced cave complex restaurant. Walk 10 minutes into Postojna town for proper local lunch at Gostilna Požar — good Slovenian food, normal prices. Daily lunch special (malica) is usually €7–9 including soup.
€8–11
Afternoon
Predjama Castle
Predjama (10km from Postojna — taxi or shuttle)
A castle literally built into the mouth of a cave in a 123m cliff. One of the most dramatic buildings you'll ever see. The Postojna-to-Predjama shuttle runs when the cave has tours. Alternatively, a taxi from Postojna costs around €10 each way — split with other travelers at the cave exit.
Shuttle: €8 return · Castle: €14
Dinner
Back in Ljubljana — Lunch Café Marley & Me
Stari trg 9, Old Town
After a long day, this cozy spot in the old town does excellent burgers, sandwiches, and local craft beer. Laid-back vibe, fair prices, outdoor seating by the river when weather allows.
Bus: €6.80 · Dinner: €10–14
🚌 Transport tip: Arriva buses run Ljubljana–Postojna roughly hourly. Leave Ljubljana by 9am to fit cave + castle comfortably before the last bus back (~7pm). Check current schedules at ap.arriva.si.
Day 05
Final Morning & Departure
~€22 Today's total
Morning
Rooster Coffee + Zmajski Most (Dragon Bridge)
Rooster Coffee: Trubarjeva cesta 52
Ljubljana has excellent coffee culture. Rooster is a local specialty café doing proper filter and espresso. After coffee, walk across the Dragon Bridge — four copper dragons, symbol of Ljubljana. Take the photos you forgot earlier in the trip.
Coffee: €3
Lunch
Last Slovenian Meal — Restavracija Manna
Eipprova ulica 1a
Manna does Slovenian cuisine with a slight modern twist — great value lunch menu. Or go back to the Central Market for one final burek and fruit. Grab some Slovenian chocolate (Zima brand) as a souvenir.
€10–14
Afternoon
Airport Transfer
Ljubljana City Centre → LJU Airport
Arriva airport bus line 28G departs from in front of the train station (Trg Osvobodilne fronte). Check current schedule at ap.arriva.si. Journey is 45 min — allow 2.5 hours before your flight.
Bus: €4.10
💡 The bus goes directly to the terminal. Much better value than the €35+ taxi. Just don't cut it too close to your flight.
✈️ Departure tip: Ljubljana Airport is small and fast — 30–40 min from check-in to gate. Don't overcompensate by arriving 3 hours early. 1.5 hours is genuinely enough.
Save Money

10 ways to stretch
your €500 further

01
Eat lunch at the Central Market — Vendors sell fresh produce, cheese, and cooked food at local prices. Your best cheap meal of the trip is here.
02
Walk around Lake Bled instead of renting a bike — The loop takes 1.5 hours, it's flat, and it's completely free. The bike rental (€8/hr) isn't worth it.
03
Book Postojna Cave online in advance — Saves you queue time but also sometimes has small early-bird discounts. Prices at the door are the same but slower.
04
Use the Arriva airport bus both ways — €4.10 vs €35 taxi. The bus is comfortable, direct, and reliable. The taxi saving alone covers a full day of food.
05
Drink tap water — Ljubljana tap water is mountain spring quality. Seriously one of the best tap waters in Europe. Never buy bottled water here.
06
Visit Metelkova at night — The alternative arts quarter is free, always interesting, and often has live events. Best free nightlife in the city.
07
Hike up to Ljubljana Castle — The funicular is €4 each way. The walk through the park takes 20 min and is actually more enjoyable. Save the €8 for a good dinner.
08
Buy a supermarket breakfast — Mercator or Spar near any hostel. Coffee + yoghurt + pastry = €3. Hostel breakfast "deals" are usually €6–8 for the same thing.
09
Combine Postojna + Predjama in one day — They're 10km apart. A Postojna-only day wastes a bus fare. Always do both on the same trip.
10
Use Bolt for taxis instead of street cabs — Ljubljana taxis are regulated but Bolt is slightly cheaper and always metered. Download before you arrive.

Watch Out

Mistakes that will waste
your time or money

✗
Eating at restaurants directly on the Bled lakefront. These are tourist traps with 40–60% markup over normal Ljubljana prices. Walk one block back or use local spots like Penzion Mlino.
✗
Taking a taxi from Ljubljana Airport. They charge €35–45 for a route the bus does for €4.10. The bus is comfortable, direct, and 45 minutes. There's zero reason for a taxi unless you have excessive luggage.
✗
Going to Bled without going early. After 11am in summer it's overcrowded. The same view that looks like a screensaver at 8am looks like a packed car park at noon.
✗
Skipping Predjama Castle. Almost every visitor does Postojna Cave and skips the castle 10km away. It's arguably more impressive and equally unique. Always do both.
✗
Not trying kremšnita in Bled. This is the one non-negotiable food experience. The original recipe at Slaščičarna Zima. Don't leave without it.
